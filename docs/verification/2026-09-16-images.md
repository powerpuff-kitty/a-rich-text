## Boundaries

The upload callback is host-owned; tests use controlled providers rather than
an external storage service. Physical-device orientation, real IME and manual
screen-reader validation remain open. Cancellation aborts the request and ignores
late results, but server-side asset cleanup belongs to the integrating host.
See [image integration](../image-authoring.md) and [remaining work](../remaining-work.md).
